12 Years at the Hearth
The Beginning

Started with an 80-year-old brass mortar & two cords of white oak.

In 2012, Chef Arash unpacked his grandmother's hand-hammered brass mortar from Isfahan, built an open clay hearth by hand, and opened Saffron Table with twelve wooden chairs. No foam, no tweezers, no culinary smoke and mirrors.

Every morning starts at 6 AM: stone-grinding dried barberries, toasting wild mountain cumin, and steeping crimson saffron threads in ice water before finishing them over glowing oak charcoal.

Clay Oven & Live Fire

Flatbreads slapped onto scorching terracotta walls and served hot with whipped sheep's feta.

Single-Origin Sargol Saffron

Pure red stigmas sourced directly from heritage cooperatives in Khorasan—never dyed, never blended.

Low-Intervention Natural Cellar

Skin-contact orange wines, chilled Mediterranean reds, and saffron-smoked cocktails on hand-cut ice.

Our Story

Twelve Years at the Hearth

From twelve wooden chairs to a Michelin-recommended kitchen.

2012

The Founding

Chef Arash opens Saffron Table with 12 chairs, a clay hearth built by hand, and his grandmother's brass mortar from Isfahan.

2014

First Press Recognition

Named "Best New Restaurant" by Table & Vine magazine. The lamb shank sells out every night for three months.

2017

The Natural Cellar Opens

We expand with a low-intervention wine cellar — 140 labels across skin-contact, pét-nat, and biodynamic producers.

2020

Michelin Recommendation

Saffron Table earns its first Michelin mention — "A masterclass in hearth cooking."

2024

The Counter Expansion

We add 8 open-kitchen counter seats — front-row heat from the oak hearth.

What We Stand For

Our Three Commitments

Not buzzwords — actual decisions we make every single morning before service.

Provenance First

Every ingredient is traceable. Our saffron comes from a single Khorasan cooperative. Our barberries are wild-foraged. No shortcuts.

Live Fire Only

We do not own a microwave. Everything — proteins, vegetables, flatbreads — passes through open white oak flame or clay oven.

Genuine Hospitality

We remember your name and your usual order. Persian hospitality isn't a brand strategy — it's how we were raised.
