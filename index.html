12 Years at the Hearth
Our Heritage

Started with an 80-year-old brass mortar & two cords of white oak.

In 2012, Chef Arash unpacked his grandmother's hand-hammered brass mortar from Isfahan, built an open clay hearth by hand, and opened Saffron Table with twelve wooden chairs. No foam, no tweezers, no culinary smoke and mirrors.

Every morning starts at 6 AM: stone-grinding dried barberries, toasting wild mountain cumin, and steeping crimson saffron threads in ice water before finishing them over glowing oak charcoal. What arrives at your table is honest, unhurried, and deeply personal.
